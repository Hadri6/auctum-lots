Read first – what is complete in this lot

Minet sells a bed in two ways: as a complete bed (“LIT”, headboard included) or as a bed frame with a headboard bought separately. Both kinds are in this stock, so not every bed line is a whole bed. The table counts what is complete and what can be put together from this lot alone.

Complete beds – headboard included
Aria, Thalya, Calypso, Eden, Pégase, Epsilon and end-of-series models (“LIT” lines). Mattress and slatted base not included.
145

Wave beds + Wave headboards – finished with colour panels from this lot

Galaxie bed frames (no headboard)
